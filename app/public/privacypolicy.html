Sapna Health and your health data

Sapna Health is a private app made for one person. This page explains what it does with data from Health Connect.

What it reads

  • Steps — your daily step count (for example from Samsung Health), so you don’t have to type it in.

It reads nothing else, and it never writes to Health Connect.

Where it goes

  • Your steps are saved on this phone with the rest of your log.
  • If you turned on sharing with Rishu, your daily totals are sent to him end-to-end encrypted — only his devices can read them.
  • Nothing is sold, used for ads, or shared with anyone else.

Turning it off

Settings → Steps from Samsung Health → Disconnect, or remove the permission in Health Connect at any time. Steps already saved stay in your log.
